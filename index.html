Strategic Quickstart

Project docs, pre-made.

Set up the project, tick the documents you need, adjust the phases. Export drops a complete, pre-filled set on your computer: workplan, Gantt chart, RACI matrix, charter, risk register, and more.

No accounts, no sign-in, no tracking. Your documents are built in the browser and never leave it. Works offline once loaded.

Start typing a name to pick from the roster: role and initials fill in automatically. Initials become the owner dropdown in every spreadsheet, names become the RACI columns.

3. Phases and tasks

A phase is a slice of your timeline. Its weight decides how big that slice is: a phase weighted 20 out of a total of 100 gets a fifth of the horizon. Tasks inside a phase are then laid end to end across its slice, so each one gets a start and a due date.

  • One task per line. Press Enter for the next one.
  • Start a line with * for a milestone: no duration, pinned to the end of its phase, and marked ◆ in the plan and Gantt.
  • Leave Owner blank and the phase inherits the project owner.
  • The grey line under each phase shows the dates your weight produced. Nothing is locked in: edit any of it, or fix the dates in the spreadsheet afterwards.
