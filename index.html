3. Phases and tasks

A phase is a slice of your timeline. Its weight decides how big that slice is: a phase weighted 20 out of a total of 100 gets a fifth of the horizon. Tasks inside a phase are then laid end to end across its slice, so each one gets a start and a due date.

  • One task per line. Press Enter for the next one.
  • Start a line with * for a milestone: no duration, pinned to the end of its phase, and marked ◆ in the plan and Gantt.
  • Leave Owner blank and the phase inherits the project owner.
  • The grey line under each phase shows the dates your weight produced. Nothing is locked in: edit any of it, or fix the dates in the spreadsheet afterwards.

Two URLs and this becomes no-login

Right now posting needs a GitHub account. To let anyone add a note without signing in, put a Google Form in front of it. Roughly four minutes, no code:

  1. Create a Google Form. Suggested questions: Title (short answer), What is awkward or missing (paragraph), What you would like instead (paragraph), Which document or step (short answer), How much it matters (multiple choice), Your name, optional (short answer).
  2. In Settings, make sure it does not require sign-in and does not collect email addresses. That is what keeps it anonymous.
  3. Send → the link icon → untick Shorten URL, and copy the long /viewform URL.
  4. On the Responses tab, link it to a Google Sheet. In that sheet: File → Share → Publish to web, pick the response tab, choose Comma-separated values (.csv), and Publish. Copy that URL.
  5. Paste both into assets/notes-config.js and push. The tab switches over on its own.

Anything submitted through the form is readable by anyone who opens this page, so treat it as a public suggestion box rather than somewhere for anything sensitive.

Generated files are a scaffold, not a plan. The dates come from spreading template phases evenly across your window, which is arithmetic rather than judgment: walk them with the team and change what is wrong. Placeholders appear in [square brackets]; blanks are deliberate.
