Initiative Kit

Project docs, pre-made.

Choose a template, set the owners and the timeline, tick the documents you need. Export drops a complete, pre-filled set on your computer: workplan, Gantt chart, RACI matrix, charter, risk register, and more.

Everything runs in your browser. No accounts, no uploads, no tracking. Works offline once loaded.

1. Set up the project

Team

Initials become the owner dropdown in every spreadsheet. Names become the RACI columns.

2. Phases and tasks

One task per line. Prefix a line with * to make it a milestone: zero duration, pinned to the end of its phase. Weight sets how much of the horizon a phase takes.

3. Pick your documents

Output

Preview

Generated files are a scaffold, not a plan. The dates come from spreading template phases evenly across your window, which is arithmetic rather than judgment: walk them with the team and change what is wrong. Placeholders appear in [square brackets]; blanks are deliberate.

No dependencies, no network calls. Source on GitHub.
